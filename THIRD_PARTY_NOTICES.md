The icons in `static/icons.js` and `static/icons/png/` are generated by `tools/icons.py` from these open-source icon sets:

- **Framework7 Icons** by Vladimir Kharlampidi, MIT License. https://github.com/framework7io/framework7-icons
- **Phosphor Icons** by Phosphor Icons, MIT License. https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core
- **Lucide** by the Lucide contributors, ISC License. https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide

At runtime the project can use these free services (no account needed): weather from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/)
(CC BY 4.0 data), synced lyrics from [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net/), and pictures and summaries from
[Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/) for answer cards. OpenAI and Home Assistant are used only if you configure them.

The pop-ups use the San Francisco fonts that ship with macOS (`/System/Library/Fonts`). They are read from your Mac at
runtime and are not included in this repository.
